40 MW Margibi Solar Power Project

Harnessing Liberia's solar potential to strengthen domestic electricity supply.

Phoenix Services Incorporated is advancing the development of a proposed 40 MW utility-scale solar photovoltaic power project in Margibi County, Liberia.

The proposed facility is located along the Robertsfield Highway near Ambush Curve, approximately 10 miles from Roberts International Airport.

The project is intended to provide additional renewable generation capacity to Liberia's electricity system and is being developed with experienced regional technical and financing partners.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Renewable capacity for a growing system.

The proposed project is intended to contribute new renewable generation capacity to Liberia's electricity system and support the country's broader objectives of increasing reliable electricity supply, diversifying generation and expanding access to power.

Phoenix is pursuing the project as an Independent Power Producer development, with the Liberia Electricity Corporation envisioned as the long-term electricity off-taker, subject to negotiation and execution of definitive agreements.

WHY MARGIBI?

A strategic corridor.

Margibi County occupies a strategically important position within Liberia's economic and transportation corridor. The county's proximity to Roberts International Airport, major commercial activity, growing communities and the national electricity network presents an opportunity to develop energy infrastructure that can support both existing and future electricity demand.

The proposed project is also being developed within the broader context of increasing electricity access and economic activity across Margibi County.

FINANCING

An indicative, evolving estimate.

Phoenix is engaging with prospective technical and financing partners regarding the development and financing of the project.

The preliminary estimated investment of approximately US$24 million, based on approximately US$600,000 per MW for 40 MW, is indicative only and will be refined through engineering, procurement, feasibility and financing work.

Final cost will depend on plant design, equipment selection, grid interconnection and transmission requirements, land development, battery or grid-support requirements, environmental and social requirements, financing costs, EPC terms, market conditions and other project-development factors.

ELECTRICITY ACCESS IN MARGIBI COUNTY

Responsible development, broadly framed.

Phoenix recognizes the importance of expanding electricity access throughout Margibi County. The proposed solar project is being developed within a broader environment of growing electricity demand and efforts to connect communities, commercial customers and industrial users to reliable electricity.

Phoenix welcomes constructive collaboration with county authorities, communities, LEC and other stakeholders where such collaboration can support responsible energy development and expanded access to electricity.
