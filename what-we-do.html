WHAT WE DO

Four principal areas of work.

Energy & Power, Infrastructure & Engineering, Project Development, and Strategic Investment & Partnerships.

01 — ENERGY & POWER

An emerging energy platform.

Phoenix is developing an emerging energy platform focused on opportunities that can contribute to Liberia's electricity supply and energy security.

  • Utility-scale solar
  • Renewable energy
  • Power generation
  • Energy infrastructure
  • Transmission & grid infrastructure
  • Battery energy storage
  • Distributed electricity solutions
  • Energy-enabled economic development
Current flagship energy initiative

40 MW Margibi Solar Power Project — Status: Under Development

02 — INFRASTRUCTURE & ENGINEERING

Building alongside partners.

Phoenix is positioned to participate in infrastructure development, engineering and construction-related opportunities. Projects may be undertaken directly or through qualified technical and implementation partners.

  • Civil infrastructure
  • Electrical infrastructure
  • Power infrastructure
  • General contracting
  • Engineering services
  • Construction management
  • Project supervision
  • Infrastructure development
03 — PROJECT DEVELOPMENT

From opportunity to investable project.

Phoenix's role is to bring together the different elements required to turn an opportunity into an investable project, coordinating among commercial, technical, regulatory, environmental, financial and institutional stakeholders.

Opportunity Identification→ Site→ Technical Partner→ Off-taker→ Financing→ Feasibility→ Approvals→ Implementation
04 — STRATEGIC INVESTMENT & PARTNERSHIPS

We seek to work in collaboration with others.

We work with partners whose expertise, capital, technology or institutional relationships can strengthen project development. Phoenix welcomes engagement with:

  • Investors
  • Development finance institutions
  • Commercial lenders
  • EPC contractors
  • Engineering firms
  • Renewable energy companies
  • Government institutions
  • Utilities & strategic investors

"Our local role is complemented by specialized partners where a project requires technical expertise, financing, engineering, environmental and social capability, or construction capacity."
