OUR FLAGSHIP PROJECT

40 MW Margibi Solar Power Project

Under development

Phoenix Services is advancing the development of a proposed 40 MW utility-scale solar photovoltaic project along the Robertsfield Highway in Margibi County.

The project is being developed with prospective regional technical and financing partners and is intended to supply electricity to Liberia's national power system through a long-term power purchase arrangement with the Liberia Electricity Corporation.
