ABOUT PHOENIX

Who we are.

A Liberian company focused on developing and advancing strategic energy, infrastructure and commercial opportunities.

The company's corporate mandate encompasses management and business advisory services, engineering and construction, general contracting, infrastructure development, financing and the development, construction, ownership and operation of electric and solar power facilities and related energy systems.

Phoenix is building on this foundation to develop projects that respond to Liberia's infrastructure requirements while creating opportunities for investment, local participation and long-term economic value.

OUR VISION

To contribute to a stronger, more connected and economically vibrant Liberia through responsible project development and investment.

OUR MISSION

To identify, develop and facilitate commercially viable projects that address Liberia's critical infrastructure and energy needs while creating lasting value for communities, investors and national development.

OUR VALUES

What we hold to.

Integrity

We believe credibility is earned through transparency, accountability and keeping our commitments.

Partnership

We believe major projects are built through collaboration among government, communities, investors, technical experts and private enterprise.

Local Value

We seek to create meaningful opportunities for Liberian participation, employment, enterprise development and skills transfer.

Practicality

We focus on projects that address real needs and can be technically, commercially and financially sustained.

Long-Term Thinking

We are interested not simply in developing projects, but in creating assets that can contribute to Liberia's development over the long term.

OUR PERSPECTIVE

Beyond announcements.

Liberia's development needs require private-sector participation alongside government and public institutions. Phoenix seeks to contribute by identifying practical opportunities, organizing the necessary partnerships and advancing projects through disciplined development processes.

Our objective is not simply to announce projects, but to develop projects that can withstand technical, commercial, financial, environmental, regulatory and institutional scrutiny.
