PARTNERSHIPS

Strong projects are built through strong partnerships.

Our model brings together local project development capacity with specialized technical, financial and institutional expertise.

Local Development & Stakeholder Engagement

Phoenix provides local project development, stakeholder engagement and knowledge of Liberia's operating environment. This includes engagement with:

  • Government institutions
  • County authorities
  • Utilities
  • Communities
  • Landowners and local stakeholders
  • Regulatory institutions
  • Commercial and institutional partners

Technical Expertise

Major energy and infrastructure projects require specialized technical capabilities. Phoenix works to engage qualified partners in areas including:

  • Renewable energy engineering
  • Solar PV design
  • Electrical engineering
  • Grid integration
  • EPC
  • Battery energy storage
  • Civil works
  • Operations and maintenance

Financial Capacity

Phoenix is open to partnerships with institutions capable of providing:

  • Equity
  • Project debt
  • Structured finance
  • Development capital
  • Development finance
  • Export credit support
  • Other appropriate project financing instruments

Institutional Partnerships

Phoenix recognizes the importance of collaboration with government institutions, utilities, development agencies and other stakeholders. The company seeks to build relationships based on transparency, clearly defined responsibilities and alignment around project objectives.

REGIONAL TECHNICAL PARTNERSHIP

Bringing regional technical capability into the Margibi project.

Where the relationship is formally documented and approved for public disclosure, Phoenix may identify Zanet Energy of Ghana as a regional technical partner supporting the development of the proposed Margibi solar project.

The precise description of the relationship should reflect the executed agreement between Phoenix and Zanet and should not imply an EPC, financing, ownership or joint-venture relationship unless the relevant agreement establishes it.

"Phoenix does not seek to do everything itself. We seek to bring together the right capabilities to develop projects properly."

We believe the strongest projects combine local understanding, technical excellence, financial discipline and responsible stakeholder engagement.
