# Phoenix Website Content & Credibility Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines are intended to keep Phoenix Services Incorporated's
public website impressive, professional and commercially useful while
ensuring that all statements remain grounded in facts and the actual
stage of project development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Describe Phoenix Accurately

Preferred positioning:

> Phoenix Services Incorporated is a Liberian company developing
> strategic energy, infrastructure and commercial opportunities.

Avoid describing Phoenix as a major or leading solar developer unless
that statement can be supported by a documented track record.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Be Precise About the 40 MW Project

Preferred:

-   Proposed 40 MW Margibi Solar Power Project
-   40 MW Solar PV Project under development
-   Phoenix is advancing development of a proposed 40 MW project
-   Proposed long-term electricity off-take arrangement with LEC

Avoid:

-   Phoenix's operating 40 MW solar plant
-   Phoenix's existing solar portfolio
-   Phoenix's completed 40 MW project
-   40 MW plant already supplying LEC
-   Financially closed project, unless financial close has actually
    occurred

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Use Project Status Labels

Recommended status labels:

-   Concept
-   Under Development
-   Feasibility
-   Off-take Development
-   Financing
-   Financial Close
-   Construction
-   Commissioning
-   Commercial Operation

Use the status that reflects the actual project position.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Use Third-Party Images Carefully

If a photograph does not depict a Phoenix project, label it as:

> Illustrative image

or:

> Representative solar technology

Do not use photographs of another company's project in a way that could
imply ownership or operation by Phoenix.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Partners

Only use a partner's:

-   Name
-   Logo
-   Description
-   Project role

after the relationship and public disclosure have been appropriately
authorized.

Do not call a company an:

-   EPC contractor
-   Financing partner
-   Joint-venture partner
-   Technical partner
-   Project owner

unless the relevant agreement supports that description.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Financial Information

The preliminary project investment estimate is approximately US\$24
million based on an indicative US\$600,000 per MW for a 40 MW project.

This should always be described as:

> Preliminary estimated investment, subject to detailed technical,
> financial and commercial assessment.

The final cost may change based on equipment, EPC terms, grid
infrastructure, battery storage, financing, land, environmental and
social requirements and other project-development factors.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. PPA Language

Preferred:

> Phoenix is pursuing a long-term power purchase arrangement with the
> Liberia Electricity Corporation.

Until a definitive PPA is executed, avoid:

> LEC has agreed to purchase the power.

If a Letter of Intent, term sheet, MOU or other document is executed,
describe exactly what that document establishes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Government Relationships

Government and county relationships should be described according to
their actual legal and institutional status.

Do not imply:

-   Government ownership
-   Government financing
-   Government guarantees
-   Government endorsement
-   Formal partnership
-   Exclusivity

unless documented.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Operation Lighting Margibi

If Phoenix publicly references Operation Lighting Margibi, the website
should clearly distinguish the county's electricity-access initiative
from Phoenix's solar project.

Recommended language:

> Phoenix recognizes the importance of expanding electricity access
> throughout Margibi County and welcomes constructive collaboration with
> county authorities, communities and LEC in support of responsible
> energy development and expanded access to electricity.

Any stronger description should be used only after the nature of the
collaboration has been formally established.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Corporate Documentation

The website may state that Phoenix is a Liberian company duly organized
under Liberian law.

Sensitive corporate documents should not be published unless there is a
clear business reason to do so.

Potential documents for a secure investor or LEC data room include:

-   Articles of Incorporation
-   Current Business Registration Certificate
-   Good Standing documentation
-   Tax Clearance Certificate
-   Ownership/shareholding information
-   Beneficial ownership information
-   Director and management profiles
-   Partner agreements
-   Site-control documentation
-   Project studies
-   Financial model
-   Draft commercial documents

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11. Website Tone

The Phoenix website should feel:

-   Professional
-   Confident
-   Human
-   Investment-oriented
-   Development-focused
-   Liberian
-   Internationally credible

Avoid:

-   Excessive corporate jargon
-   Unsupported superlatives
-   Inflated claims
-   Generic "industry leader" language
-   Artificially impressive statistics
-   Claims based solely on future intentions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 12. The Core Phoenix Story

The website should consistently communicate six ideas:

1.  Phoenix is a legitimate Liberian company.
2.  Its corporate mandate includes energy and infrastructure
    development.
3.  Phoenix understands Liberia's operating environment.
4.  Phoenix is developing a specific 40 MW solar project in Margibi
    County.
5.  Phoenix is bringing qualified technical and financial partners into
    the project.
6.  Phoenix is building a broader infrastructure and energy platform
    over time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 13. The Desired Visitor Takeaway

After reviewing the website, an LEC official, investor or technical
partner should understand:

> Phoenix is a legitimate Liberian project sponsor with a defined energy
> project, a credible development process, local stakeholder capability
> and an emerging network of technical and financial partners.

That is the credibility position Phoenix should build publicly while its
first major energy project moves toward feasibility, contracting and
financing.
